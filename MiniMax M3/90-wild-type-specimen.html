Wild Type Specimen

a typographic herbarium pressed & live

This is a typographic herbarium; it grows in a slow, attentive garden. Each letter is given room; each letter is encouraged to settle.

The g is the greenest letter — it tends sideways, like a small plant. The t is a stick-in-the-mud; it does not lean.

Set in its own season, the S curls into a swallow before it lands.

The m is a hillside; the w is two hillsides; the y, impatient, with its tail up.

We press the B for its taxonomy: round, bold, slightly ill-tempered. The Q has always preferred tails to heads.

The . — full stop — is the smallest plant. It survives every winter. It is the seed of the next sentence.

The ! — exclamation — is a stiff plant from the Mediterranean. Plant sparingly.

The ? — question — is taller than it appears. Look at its hook.
