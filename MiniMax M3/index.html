Process

One hundred pages, written one at a time. No templates, no shared layouts. Each entry has its own rhythm, palette, and posture. Generously mixed across disciplines — almanacs, atlases, dashboards, field notes, presses, drawers, boats — and yet somehow, all the same quiet.

How to read

Each design opens in its own tab. Every page is fully self-contained — no external dependencies, no tracking. Click them out of order. Read three, then seventeen, then two. The kettle rewards no particular sequence.

Note on motion

Some pages animate. Each honours prefers-reduced-motion; the rest is courtesy. A small number include flashing light or cyclical strobing; step away if the cadence bothers you.
