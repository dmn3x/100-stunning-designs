Driver's note

The 22:14 carries mail, parcels, two sleeping cars, and the dining car. We're held for thirteen at Bletchley — a freight train has lost its tail lamp.

The line is single between Leighton and Tring. Proceed cautiously. Do not exceed 60 mph until Cheddington.

On arrival at Watford, the parcels dispatcher — Mr. Anders — will require your signature for the consignment marked handle with care: glass instruments.

You will be relieved at Crewe. Sleep, if you can.
