Commonplace Book

scrapbook of borrowed lines vol. iv
from a bus ticket, Margate, 1998

On patience

"The sea is also a kind of waiting room; only the chairs keep moving."

— annotation: I do not yet believe this, but I do not disbelieve it either.
letter to L., undelivered, 2003

On friendship

"Friendship is not the easy thing; it is the only thing that survives."

— posted on the kitchen fridge; the magnet held it through two moves.
from H. Pul's notebook, recovered

On rivers

"When the river runs inland, it does not announce itself. It simply tells one small friend."

— Pul kept all rivers alphabetised in his later years.
telegram, received 14:14

On small kindnesses

"Salt, sugar, lemon — keep these in order. So too with kindnesses."

— from the assistant librarian; the original telegram is in the museum.
cocktail napkin, Hotel Verdigris

On hotels

"A hotel is a house that promises, without exception, to forget you."

— I always check in with two names.
playbill, third row

On theatre

"A play is a rehearsal you forgot to schedule for."

— from the program of "A Slow Conversation in the Dunes."
postcard from the lake

On listening

"The lake looks like an ear. People listen anyway."

— I keep this for the symmetry.
