Tactile Industrial Manual

Soft Machinery manufactures feelings: pressure, warmth, the small click of certainty. Read the operator's notes before turning the levers.

  • P — Pressure (kPa). Hold at 12; never exceed 30.
  • H — Heat (°C). Warm wool is patient wool.
  • C — Cycle (ms). Slow the machine before stopping it.
  • ! — If the cog skips, gently refuse. The cog knows.

— a note from the service technician, signed in felt-tip.
