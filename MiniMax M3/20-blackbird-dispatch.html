Civic affairs · 03:14

Council moves to ban outdoor reading past ten p.m.

By E. Marlowe · 03:14 · Stonebridge

The Stonebridge city council voted six to three last night to introduce an ordinance prohibiting recreational reading in public spaces after the ten o'clock siren. Councillor F. Vell, who proposed the motion, said readers were "a drain on lampposts" and "inconveniently quiet."

An impromptu gathering of readers outside the courthouse, organised by the Blackbird Library and held, fittingly, in silence, dispersed at 22:07. The mayor declined comment. The librarian declined to comment. The ducks declined to comment, though one was observed to quack.

Hard news · 02:40

River observed running backwards; or, maybe, ordinary reversal

By an unattributed source · Marsden

A mariner at the Marsden wharf reports that the river turned and ran inland for twelve minutes before resuming its normal course. Eyewitnesses counted three fish swimming north.

The harbourmaster attributed the phenomenon to "the wind", but declined to clarify which direction it had blown from. Operators of small vessels were advised to remain in port for the remainder of the evening.
