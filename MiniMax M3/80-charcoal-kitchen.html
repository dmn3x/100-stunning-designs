Charcoal Kitchen — Tarte Tatin

Take eight small apples. Cut them in halves and then in halves again. Caramelise slowly. Lay a disc of pastry on top. Invert. Eat with a long spoon.

The Work

  • Use a heavy iron pan.
  • Do not stir after the sugar turns.
  • Tell at least one person in the room what you are doing.

The Quiet

Tarte Tatin cannot be hurried. It does not respond to commentary. If the kitchen is too silent, bring the radio.

Chef's notes

— Always forgive the pan.

— Eat it first; photograph later.

— Send a slice to the neighbour with the loud kettle.

— Reserve the burnt bits for yourself. They are the truth.
