Raw honesty in digital form

Brutalism in digital design is not an aesthetic choice. It is a moral position. Every pixel serves a purpose. Every line of code carries weight. There is no decoration without function. No ornament without meaning.

The web was built on text. Hypertext. Links. Structure. Semantics. Somewhere along the way we buried the content under layers of chrome, shadows, gradients, animations — all screaming for attention while saying nothing.

This is a return. To structure. To hierarchy. To the raw materials of the medium: HTML, CSS, Typography. Nothing hidden. Nothing pretending to be what it is not.

Brutalist digital design exposes its construction. The grid is visible. The type is unapologetically large. The colors are system defaults or deliberate violations. Interactions are immediate, not animated into oblivion.

Content is not king. Content is the only thing that exists. Everything else is infrastructure.

Principles

  • Truth to materials
  • Structure over decoration
  • Content determines form
  • Default is a design decision
  • Accessibility is not optional
  • Performance is a feature
  • No dark patterns
  • No tracking without consent
